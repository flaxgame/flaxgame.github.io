Scootle: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Scootle ("the game") is a puzzle game for Android published by Flaxgame (Sumanta Paikaray), Balugaon, Odisha, India ("we"). This policy explains what information the game and the Google services built into it handle, and the choices you have. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location, and we do not sell personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress (stars, moves, daily puzzle streak, achievements, hint balance, purchases you've made, and your settings) is saved in a file on your phone. It stays on your device unless you sign in to Google Play Games (section 3). Uninstalling the game deletes it; Settings → Reset progress erases progress at any time.

2. Information collected by Google services in the game

The game uses the following Google services. They collect information automatically under Google's own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy), and we receive only aggregated or pseudonymous reports from them.

ServiceWhat it collectsWhy
Google AdMob (ads) and the User Messaging Platform (consent) Advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate a general location, such as the country), device and app information, and how you interact with ads Showing ads, measuring them, limiting how often they appear, and preventing fraud. Ads can be personalised only where the law allows it and, where required, only with your consent
Google Analytics for Firebase A random app-instance ID, device model, OS version, country, and gameplay events (for example level started, completed or quit, moves, time taken, hints used, ads shown, purchases) Understanding which levels are too hard or unclear and improving the game
Firebase Crashlytics Crash reports: the state of the app and device when the crash happened, and a random installation ID Finding and fixing crashes
Google Play Games Services (optional sign-in) Your Play Games player ID and gamer name, scores, achievements, and a copy of your saved progress Leaderboards, achievements and cloud save across your devices
Google Play Billing Purchase details are processed by Google Play. The game only receives which item you bought and a purchase token Delivering what you bought and restoring purchases

Data sent by these services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Analytics data is kept for 2 months (the Firebase retention setting), and then deleted automatically.

3. Cloud save

If you sign in to Google Play Games, your saved progress is stored by Google in a hidden area of your Google account so it can be restored on another phone. You can delete it from the Play Games app (Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, or delete this game's data there).

4. Your choices

  • Ad privacy choices: where consent is required (for example in the EEA and UK), you choose when you first open the game. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android phones).
  • Remove Ads: one in-app purchase turns off the ads between levels. Watching an ad for a hint is always optional.
  • Delete your data: uninstall the game to remove local data. To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your app-instance ID, email sumanta.codex@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the game and you want their data deleted, contact us at the address above.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or US state laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. To use any of these rights, email us. We don't hold your name or contact details, so analytics data is linked only to a random ID on your phone. Tell us your phone model and roughly when you played, and we'll find and delete what we can. Uninstalling the game and resetting your Advertising ID also cuts the link for good.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be announced in the game's release notes.
