Prism Bloom: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Prism Bloom ("the game") is made by Flaxgame (Sumanta Paikaray), Balugaon, Odisha, India. This policy explains what the game does with your information.

The short version

The game does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics in this version, and it doesn't use the internet.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: levels solved, stars, daily streak, hints and settings (sound, music, vibration, colorblind glyphs, beam brightness).
  • These are saved only in the game's private storage on your phone. We can't see them. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

What we collect

Nothing. The game:

  • doesn't ask for or read your name, email, contacts, photos, location or device identifiers;
  • has no internet permission, so it can't send anything anywhere;
  • shows no advertising and includes no third-party ad, analytics or tracking code.

The only Android permission it uses is vibration, for gentle haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.

Children

The game is a family-friendly puzzle game and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because the game collects nothing at all.

Google Play

You download the game through Google Play. Google's own handling of your data (for example your Play account and purchases) is covered by Google's Privacy Policy, not this one.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example ads, in-app purchases, cloud saves or crash reports), we'll update this policy before that version is released and describe exactly what is collected, why, and how you can opt out. The date at the top shows the latest version.
