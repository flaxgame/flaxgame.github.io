About the game

The forest has faded to grey. A little fairy still holds the last light. Drag mirrors, prisms, filters and half-mirrors into her beam, turn them freely, and send the right color to each sleeping blossom. Every flower you wake brings color, glow and music back to the forest.

  • 150 hand-checked puzzles across 5 painted regions
  • A new daily puzzle every day
  • Free 360° rotation with gentle aim assist
  • Colorblind-friendly: every color also has a shape
  • No account, plays offline
